A memetic differential evolution algorithm for searching efficient neural network
architectures across accuracy, parameter count, and inference latency. We benchmark
on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet, achieving Pareto-optimal architectures that
outperform NSGA-Net and ENAS on multiple objectives.
