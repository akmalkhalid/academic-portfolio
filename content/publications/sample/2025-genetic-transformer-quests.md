We propose a novel framework that couples genetic algorithms with transformer-based
narrative generation to produce coherent, balanced quests for procedurally generated
RPGs. The genetic layer enforces structural constraints (quest length, difficulty
curve, reward distribution) while the transformer layer generates flavor text and
dialogue.
