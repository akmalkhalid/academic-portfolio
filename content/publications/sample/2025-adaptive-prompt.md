This paper proposes a hybrid framework that combines transformer-based language
models with structured medical ontologies to produce explainable diagnostic
suggestions. Our approach addresses the well-known opacity of LLM-generated medical
advice by routing model outputs through a rule-based verification layer trained on
SNOMED-CT.
