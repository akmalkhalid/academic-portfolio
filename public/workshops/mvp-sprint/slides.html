Point of view (POV)

[User] needs a way to [need] because [insight].

e.g. Off-campus final-year students need a way to secure a room before they travel in, because walking in at 2 pm usually fails.

How might we… ? (HMW)

How might we help [user] [do the need] without [today’s pain]?

e.g. How might we help them book a room from home without racing to the faculty?

Five modes after the Stanford d.school; POV and “How might we” are standard design-thinking tools. Dark pills = what today covers.

Guardrails

How not to get caught out by judges

No real personal data

No IC, phone or matric numbers. Sample data only (PDPA 2010).

Free projects can be public

Never paste API keys or private UKM data into a prompt.

The app is not the startup

An hour-long build is easy to copy. Your edge: user insight, data, channel.

Keep your prompts

Tools change fast. Your brief and prompts rebuild the app anywhere.

Say what is real.

your pitch line

“Booking works end to end. Payments are mocked. 2 of 3 testers booked without help.”
