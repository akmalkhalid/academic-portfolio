The Hook · Why It MattersPencetus · Mengapa Ia Penting

The portfolio was due in week 18. The course ended in week 12.

Portfolio perlu dihantar pada minggu 18. Kursus tamat pada minggu 12.

It was in the approved paperwork the whole time. The workload sheet scheduled a large portfolio in the examination weeks, while every teaching topic sat inside a twelve-week block — so the material a student needed for their biggest assessment stopped arriving six weeks before the assessment did. Nobody had noticed, because no single document showed both facts on the same page. The moment those two tables were compiled into one specification, the gap was impossible to miss.

Ia berada dalam dokumen yang diluluskan sepanjang masa. Helaian beban kerja menjadualkan portfolio besar pada minggu peperiksaan, sementara setiap topik pengajaran berada dalam blok dua belas minggu — jadi bahan yang diperlukan pelajar untuk pentaksiran terbesar mereka berhenti tiba enam minggu sebelum pentaksiran itu. Tiada siapa perasan, kerana tiada satu dokumen pun menunjukkan kedua-dua fakta pada halaman yang sama. Sebaik dua jadual itu dikompil menjadi satu spesifikasi, jurang itu mustahil untuk dilepaskan.

Why the arithmetic is the pointMengapa aritmetik itu intinya

Credit values in an outcome-based system are defined by notional learning hours: the time a typical learner needs across contact, independent study and assessment. Quality frameworks therefore require that the hours in your topic table and the hours in your assessment table add up to the credit you claim.1 That single sum is the cheapest audit in curriculum design — and the one most often skipped, because the two halves live in different tables.

Nilai kredit dalam sistem berasaskan hasil ditakrifkan oleh jam pembelajaran nosional: masa yang diperlukan pelajar tipikal merentas pertemuan, pembelajaran kendiri dan pentaksiran. Justeru rangka kerja jaminan kualiti menuntut agar jam dalam jadual topik dan jam dalam jadual pentaksiran anda berjumlah kepada kredit yang anda dakwa.1 Jumlah tunggal itu ialah audit termurah dalam reka bentuk kurikulum — dan yang paling kerap dilangkau, kerana dua bahagiannya berada dalam jadual berbeza.

Core Concept · Konsep TerasKonsep Teras

Compile, don't summarise

Kompil, jangan ringkaskan

DefinitionTakrifan

A summary shortens a document while staying faithful to its shape. A specification discards the document's shape entirely and reorganises its content into the fields a builder needs, in a fixed order, with every value stated once. The Spec Card is a specification. That is why it can govern eleven later sessions, and why a "please summarise my syllabus" prompt cannot.

Sebuah ringkasan memendekkan dokumen sambil setia kepada bentuknya. Sebuah spesifikasi membuang bentuk dokumen itu sepenuhnya dan menyusun semula kandungannya ke dalam medan yang diperlukan pembina, dalam susunan tetap, dengan setiap nilai dinyatakan sekali. Kad Spesifikasi ialah satu spesifikasi. Itulah sebabnya ia boleh mengawal sebelas sesi berikutnya, dan sebabnya gesaan “tolong ringkaskan sukatan saya” tidak boleh.

❌ A summary of your syllabus

❌ Ringkasan sukatan anda

Follows the source order, keeps the source headings, paraphrases the outcome statements, and silently drops whatever looked repetitive. Ask it a direct question — “how many hours does topic 4 get?” — and you have to go back to the original.

Mengikut susunan sumber, mengekalkan tajuk sumber, memparafrasa pernyataan hasil, dan senyap-senyap menggugurkan apa yang kelihatan berulang. Tanya soalan langsung — “berapa jam untuk topik 4?” — dan anda terpaksa kembali kepada dokumen asal.

✅ A specification of your course

✅ Spesifikasi kursus anda

Fixed fields in a fixed order. Outcomes quoted verbatim because they are approved language. Every hour accounted for and summed. Contradictions surfaced as flags rather than smoothed away. Answers questions without reopening the source.

Medan tetap dalam susunan tetap. Hasil pembelajaran dipetik verbatim kerana ia bahasa yang diluluskan. Setiap jam diambil kira dan dijumlahkan. Percanggahan didedahkan sebagai penanda dan bukan dilicinkan. Menjawab soalan tanpa membuka semula sumber.

The verbatim rulePeraturan verbatim

One field must never be compiled at all: the course outcomes are quoted word for word. They are approved language, and a paraphrase — however elegant — will not survive an audit that compares your material against the document on file.2

Satu medan tidak boleh dikompil langsung: hasil pembelajaran kursus dipetik perkataan demi perkataan. Ia bahasa yang diluluskan, dan parafrasa — betapa elegan sekalipun — tidak akan bertahan dalam audit yang membandingkan bahan anda dengan dokumen yang difailkan.2

Which document fills which field

Example with Variations · Contoh & VariasiContoh & Variasi

Expanding six topics into twelve units

Mengembangkan enam topik menjadi dua belas unit

Base caseKes asas

An approved syllabus lists six topics; the timetable has twelve teaching weeks. They do not divide evenly, and they are not supposed to: topic 1 is an introduction that needs one week, while topic 4 carries six hours of contact time and clearly needs three. The map is where you make that judgement explicitly — unit by unit, with the focus outcome and the hours named — instead of leaving it to be improvised twelve times.

Sukatan yang diluluskan menyenaraikan enam topik; jadual waktu mempunyai dua belas minggu pengajaran. Ia tidak terbahagi sama rata, dan memang tidak sepatutnya: topik 1 ialah pengenalan yang memerlukan satu minggu, manakala topik 4 membawa enam jam masa pertemuan dan jelas memerlukan tiga. Peta itulah tempat anda membuat pertimbangan tersebut secara eksplisit — unit demi unit, dengan hasil fokus dan jamnya dinamakan — dan bukan membiarkannya dikarang secara spontan dua belas kali.

Variation A — a topic that must split. Variasi A — topik yang mesti dipecahkan. Six contact hours on one topic becomes three units. Give each a distinct focus outcome and a distinct framework, or learners experience it as the same lesson taught three times. Splitting a topic is a teaching decision, not a division sum. Enam jam pertemuan bagi satu topik menjadi tiga unit. Beri setiap satu hasil fokus dan kerangka yang berbeza, atau pelajar mengalaminya sebagai pelajaran sama yang diajar tiga kali. Memecahkan topik ialah keputusan pengajaran, bukan pembahagian aritmetik.
Variation B — a unit that bridges two topics. Variasi B — unit yang merentasi dua topik. Some weeks legitimately serve the end of one topic and the start of the next. Record both in the map, and make the bridging explicit in that unit's backward and forward links — otherwise the network shows a break where the teaching does not. Sesetengah minggu memang melayani penghujung satu topik dan permulaan topik seterusnya. Rekodkan kedua-duanya dalam peta, dan jadikan perentasan itu jelas dalam pautan ke belakang dan ke hadapan unit tersebut — jika tidak, rangkaian menunjukkan keretakan yang tidak wujud dalam pengajaran.
Variation C — an outcome that only one unit serves. Variasi C — hasil pembelajaran yang hanya satu unit melayaninya. If a course outcome is the focus of a single unit, it is under-taught relative to its assessment weight. The map makes this visible before you build. The taxonomy level of the outcome also sets what the self-check can be: a synthesis outcome cannot be evidenced by multiple choice.3 Jika satu hasil pembelajaran kursus hanya menjadi fokus satu unit, ia kurang diajar berbanding pemberat pentaksirannya. Peta menjadikan ini kelihatan sebelum anda membina. Aras taksonomi hasil itu juga menentukan bentuk semakan kendiri: hasil pada aras sintesis tidak boleh dibuktikan melalui aneka pilihan.3

📋 P1 — Course Spec Card
Compress your curriculum documents into one page. Run once.
TASK: Compress the attached curriculum documents into a single COURSE SPEC CARD.

This card will be pasted at the start of every future session INSTEAD of the
source documents, so it must be self-sufficient and under 700 words. Discard
anything not needed to author teaching material: approval signatures, form
instructions, grade-analysis sheets, empty rows.

Output exactly this structure, as markdown:

# SPEC - [COURSE CODE]
## Identity
Code . title (all languages) . credits . status . provider . notional learning
hours . number of units . delivery mode and the face-to-face : online :
independent ratio.

## Audience
Who they are, prior knowledge, what they will NOT have, interface language(s).

## CLOs
Per CLO: number, taxonomy code, full statement in all languages, and the
assessment instrument(s) that evidence it.

## Assessment map
Table: Instrument | Weight % | CLO(s) | Learning hours | Timing.
Weights must total 100.

## Learning-time allocation
Table: Topic | CLO | contact hours | independent hours | total. Then the grand
total, which MUST reconcile with the notional hours. If it does not reconcile,
add a heading "!! DISCREPANCY" and show the arithmetic. Do not silently adjust.

## Content spine
The official topic list, in order, all languages, with subtopics.

## Constraints
Regional/contextual lens . language requirements . the own-case rule . mandated
software or resources . anything the accrediting body requires.

## Terminology lock
The exact rendering of the 10-15 key technical terms in each language, so
wording stays identical across all units. Format: term (L1) = term (L2).

RULES
- Quote official CLO wording verbatim. Do not paraphrase approved language.
- Where a source is ambiguous or wrong, flag [CHECK: ...] rather than guessing.
- Output the card only.

Why it is shaped this wayMengapa ia berbentuk begini

  • CutNaming the rubbish explicitly (signatures, empty rows) stops the model treating form boilerplate as course content.Menamakan sampah secara jelas (tandatangan, baris kosong) menghalang model menganggap teks borang sebagai kandungan kursus.
  • Verb"Quote verbatim" protects approved CLO wording. A paraphrased outcome will not survive an accreditation audit."Petik secara verbatim" melindungi rumusan HPK yang diluluskan. Hasil pembelajaran yang diparafrasa tidak akan bertahan dalam audit akreditasi.
  • SumForcing the arithmetic to be shown surfaces real errors in the source workload sheet — which are common.Memaksa aritmetik ditunjukkan mendedahkan ralat sebenar dalam helaian beban kerja sumber — yang memang lazim.
  • LockThe terminology lock is what stops unit 9 inventing a new translation for a term fixed in unit 2.Kunci istilah inilah yang menghalang unit 9 mencipta terjemahan baharu bagi istilah yang ditetapkan pada unit 2.

🗺️ P2 — Week Map & Content Briefs
The single most valuable file in the project. Run once, verify hard.
[Paste P0, then SPEC.md, then:]

TASK: Produce the content brief document for all [N] units.

STEP 1 - THE MAP
Expand the official topics into [N] units. Some topics span several units;
some units bridge two topics. Output a table:
Unit | Title (L1) | Title (L2) | Official topic served | Focus CLO | Hours

Sequencing rules:
- Unit 1 is the hub every later unit links back to. It establishes the core
  framework and the learner's own case.
- The final unit is a synthesis capstone linking to ALL prior units and to the
  learner's practice beyond the course.
- Nothing may be used before it is introduced. State the dependency chain.
- Sum the hours and confirm they reconcile with SPEC.

STEP 2 - THE BRIEFS
For EACH unit write 150-200 words in this exact shape:

## UNIT n - [Title]
Second-language title. Focus CLO.
Outcomes: three, each with its taxonomy verb and CLO mapping.
Hook: one real, verifiable, dated story, plus one factual anchor (a statistic,
  law or named framework) with its source.
Core concept: the definition to teach, plus the ONE contrast pair that sharpens
  it (X vs not-X).
Diagram: what the visual must show - the entities and the relation between them.
Example + variations: one base scenario, three variations each changing ONE
  variable.
Activity: what the learner does with their OWN case, and with what tool.
Applications: four real domains + the required local instance.
Framework: a named, memorable, portable checklist (3-5 steps) they keep.
Self-check: the three concepts the questions must test.
Tools: four real, currently-live tools with URLs.
Sources: four real citations with DOI / arXiv / official URL. Mark [VERIFY] if
  you are not certain.
Network: earlier units it connects back to, later units it feeds, and the
  specific assessment it ties to.

GLOBAL CONSTRAINTS
- No hook story, case, tool or source may be the primary item in two units.
  Keep a running check as you write.
- Every CLO must be the focus of at least two units.
- Every assessment instrument must be seeded in at least three units.
- Assign each unit a distinct accent colour triple (accent, accent-2,
  accent-soft). Accents dark enough for white text; softs light enough for
  dark text. Output as a list.

Output the map, then the briefs, then the accent list. Nothing else.

Why it is shaped this wayMengapa ia berbentuk begini

  • MapOfficial topics rarely map one-to-one onto teaching weeks. Making the expansion explicit is where most curriculum-to-timetable errors get caught.Topik rasmi jarang dipetakan satu-ke-satu kepada minggu pengajaran. Menjadikan pengembangan ini eksplisit ialah tempat kebanyakan ralat kurikulum-ke-jadual dikesan.
  • HubDeclaring unit 1 as the hub and the last as the capstone gives the network a shape. Without it you get twelve unconnected islands.Mengisytiharkan unit 1 sebagai hab dan unit akhir sebagai kemuncak memberi rangkaian itu bentuk. Tanpanya anda dapat dua belas pulau tak berhubung.
  • DupThe no-duplicates rule is enforced here, while all units are in view. It cannot be enforced later, one session at a time.Peraturan tiada pendua dikuatkuasakan di sini, semasa semua unit kelihatan. Ia tidak boleh dikuatkuasakan kemudian, satu sesi pada satu masa.
  • Seed"Seeded in at least three units" is what turns an assessment from a bolt-on into something the course actually builds toward."Disemai dalam sekurang-kurangnya tiga unit" mengubah pentaksiran daripada tempelan kepada sesuatu yang benar-benar dibina oleh kursus.

Local lens · MalaysiaLensa tempatan · Malaysia

Malaysian course owners are unusually well placed here: the SLT sheet and Table 4 of the new-course application are, between them, already a machine-readable specification of outcomes, topics, activities and workload. Most of P1's work is reorganisation, not invention. The framework's insistence that notional hours reconcile with credits is exactly the constraint that keeps generated material honest. Treat the SLT sheet not as paperwork to be survived but as the source of truth the whole chain compiles from.

Pemilik kursus di Malaysia berada pada kedudukan yang sangat baik di sini: helaian SLT dan Jadual 4 dalam permohonan kursus baharu, secara bersama, sudah pun menjadi spesifikasi lengkap bagi hasil pembelajaran, topik, aktiviti dan beban kerja. Kebanyakan kerja P1 ialah penyusunan semula, bukan penciptaan. Anggap helaian SLT bukan sebagai kertas kerja yang perlu dilalui tetapi sebagai sumber kebenaran yang menjadi asas seluruh rantaian ini.

Practical Framework · Kerangka AmaliKerangka Amali

Five tests of a usable spec card

Lima ujian bagi kad spesifikasi yang berguna

Apply these to the card P1 just returned. Any test it fails, fix by hand before you run P2 — an error here is copied into every unit you build afterwards.

Terapkan ini pada kad yang baru dipulangkan P1. Mana-mana ujian yang gagal, betulkan secara manual sebelum anda menjalankan P2 — ralat di sini disalin ke dalam setiap unit yang anda bina selepasnya.

1
The sum test. Do the topic hours plus the assessment hours equal the notional total, and does the total match the credit? Show the arithmetic; do not accept a bare number. Ujian jumlah. Adakah jam topik campur jam pentaksiran sama dengan jumlah nosional, dan adakah jumlah itu sepadan dengan kredit? Tunjukkan aritmetiknya; jangan terima nombor kosong.
2
The verbatim test. Are the course outcomes word-for-word identical to the approved document? Compare them character by character, not by impression. Ujian verbatim. Adakah hasil pembelajaran kursus sama perkataan demi perkataan dengan dokumen yang diluluskan? Bandingkan aksara demi aksara, bukan mengikut tanggapan.
3
The evidence test. Does every assessment instrument name at least one outcome, and every outcome name at least one instrument? An orphan on either side is a design fault, not a formatting one. Ujian bukti. Adakah setiap instrumen pentaksiran menamakan sekurang-kurangnya satu hasil, dan setiap hasil menamakan sekurang-kurangnya satu instrumen? Yatim di mana-mana pihak ialah kecacatan reka bentuk, bukan kecacatan format.
4
The stranger test. Could a colleague who has never seen your course build a unit from this card alone? If they would have to ask you a question, the answer belongs in the card. Ujian orang asing. Bolehkah rakan sekerja yang tidak pernah melihat kursus anda membina satu unit daripada kad ini sahaja? Jika mereka terpaksa bertanya kepada anda, jawapannya sepatutnya berada dalam kad itu.
5
The one-page test. Is it short enough that you will actually keep pasting it? A card that grows past a page stops being pasted, and the method quietly stops working. Ujian satu halaman. Adakah ia cukup pendek sehingga anda benar-benar akan terus menampalnya? Kad yang melebihi satu halaman berhenti ditampal, dan kaedah ini senyap-senyap berhenti berfungsi.
On flagsTentang penanda

A [CHECK] or a discrepancy heading in the returned card is a success, not a failure. It means the model refused to smooth over a contradiction in your paperwork. Resolve it as a curriculum decision — then record the decision, because you will be asked about it later.

Penanda [CHECK] atau tajuk percanggahan dalam kad yang dipulangkan ialah kejayaan, bukan kegagalan. Ia bermakna model enggan melicinkan percanggahan dalam dokumen anda. Selesaikannya sebagai keputusan kurikulum — kemudian rekodkan keputusan itu, kerana anda akan ditanya mengenainya kelak.

Quick check — instant feedback

2. Which field of the spec card must not be compiled or reworded at all?2. Medan kad spesifikasi yang manakah tidak boleh dikompil atau ditulis semula langsung?
✓ Yes. Outcomes are approved language. A paraphrase — however clear — breaks the match between your material and the document on file, which is exactly what an audit compares.✓ Ya. Hasil pembelajaran ialah bahasa yang diluluskan. Parafrasa — betapa jelas sekalipun — memutuskan padanan antara bahan anda dan dokumen yang difailkan, iaitu tepat apa yang dibandingkan dalam audit.
Those are all compiled and reorganised freely. Only the course outcomes are quoted word for word, because they are the approved wording an audit will check against.Kesemuanya dikompil dan disusun semula secara bebas. Hanya hasil pembelajaran kursus dipetik perkataan demi perkataan, kerana itulah rumusan diluluskan yang akan disemak dalam audit.

✓ Exactly. Global constraints have to be applied at the only moment the whole series is visible. Afterwards the ledger can report what was used, but only P2 can plan the distribution.✓ Tepat. Kekangan global mesti diterapkan pada satu-satunya saat keseluruhan siri kelihatan. Selepas itu lejar boleh melaporkan apa yang telah digunakan, tetapi hanya P2 boleh merancang agihannya.
Think about what each later session can actually see: P0, the spec, the contract, the ledger and one brief. Nothing in that view can plan how twelve hooks should be distributed — only P2 has the whole series in front of it.Fikirkan apa yang benar-benar dapat dilihat oleh setiap sesi kemudian: P0, spesifikasi, kontrak, lejar dan satu ringkasan. Getting your documents into the session

P1 is the only prompt that needs your source files. Spreadsheets and forms travel badly, so it is worth a few minutes converting them into something the model reads cleanly.

P1 ialah satu-satunya gesaan yang memerlukan fail sumber anda. Hamparan dan borang tidak berpindah dengan baik, jadi berbaloi meluangkan beberapa minit menukarkannya kepada sesuatu yang dibaca model dengan bersih.

📊 Export sheets as CSV or markdownEksport hamparan sebagai CSV atau markdown
Merged cells and formulas confuse extraction. One flat table per sheet, headers in the first row, is read far more reliably than the original file.Sel bergabung dan formula mengelirukan pengekstrakan. Satu jadual rata bagi setiap helaian, tajuk pada baris pertama, dibaca jauh lebih boleh dipercayai daripada fail asal.
📄 Strip the form furnitureBuang perabot borang
Signature blocks, approval boxes and repeated instruction rows are noise. Deleting them before you attach measurably improves what P1 returns.Blok tandatangan, kotak kelulusan dan baris arahan berulang ialah gangguan. Memadamnya sebelum melampirkan meningkatkan hasil P1 dengan ketara.

Attach only the documents for the course you are compiling. A folder holding three courses invites the model to blend their outcomes.Lampirkan hanya dokumen untuk kursus yang sedang anda kompil. Folder yang mengandungi tiga kursus mengundang model mencampuradukkan hasil pembelajarannya.

The one job you cannot delegateSatu tugas yang tidak boleh diwakilkan

P2 will produce four citations per unit. Across twelve units that is forty-eight references, and a model cannot confirm that any of them exist. Verifying them now costs perhaps thirty minutes; discovering a fabricated source after students have cited it costs considerably more.

P2 akan menghasilkan empat petikan bagi setiap unit. Merentas dua belas unit itu empat puluh lapan rujukan, dan model tidak boleh mengesahkan bahawa mana-mana daripadanya wujud. Mengesahkannya sekarang mungkin memakan tiga puluh minit; menemui sumber palsu selepas pelajar memetiknya memakan kos yang jauh lebih besar.

Wrap-up · RumusanRumusan

Unit 2 in one breath

Unit 2 dalam satu nafas

Your approved documents already hold the specification; they just hold it in the wrong shape, spread across tables that never appear on the same page. Compiling — not summarising — reorganises that content into fixed fields with the outcomes quoted verbatim and every hour summed. The compile usually finds something: a gap in the arithmetic, an outcome no instrument evidences, an assessment scheduled after the teaching stops. That finding is the method paying for itself before you generate a single unit.

Dokumen anda yang diluluskan sudah memegang spesifikasi itu; ia cuma memegangnya dalam bentuk yang salah, bertaburan merentas jadual yang tidak pernah muncul pada halaman yang sama. Mengkompil — bukan meringkaskan — menyusun semula kandungan itu ke dalam medan tetap dengan hasil pembelajaran dipetik verbatim dan setiap jam dijumlahkan. Kompilasi itu biasanya menemui sesuatu: jurang dalam aritmetik, hasil yang tiada instrumen membuktikannya, pentaksiran yang dijadualkan selepas pengajaran tamat. Penemuan itu ialah kaedah ini membuktikan nilainya sebelum anda menjana satu unit pun.
