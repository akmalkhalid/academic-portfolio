The Hook · Why It MattersPencetus · Mengapa Ia Penting

Nobody decided to change the design. It changed anyway.

Tiada siapa memutuskan untuk mengubah reka bentuk. Ia berubah juga.

Put unit 1 and unit 9 of a generated series side by side and the difference is unmistakable — but no single step caused it. Unit 3 renamed one class. Unit 5 dropped a section that had felt repetitive. Unit 7 restated the styling in slightly tidier words. Unit 9 inherited all three. Each change was locally reasonable and individually invisible; together they produced a series that no longer looks like one course. This is the failure mode a contract exists to prevent, and it is why the template's style and script blocks are copied rather than described.

Letakkan unit 1 dan unit 9 daripada siri yang dijana secara bersebelahan dan perbezaannya jelas — tetapi tiada satu langkah pun yang menyebabkannya. Unit 3 menamakan semula satu kelas. Unit 5 menggugurkan bahagian yang terasa berulang. Unit 7 menyatakan semula gaya itu dengan perkataan yang sedikit lebih kemas. Unit 9 mewarisi ketiga-tiganya. Setiap perubahan munasabah secara setempat dan tidak kelihatan secara individu; bergabung, ia menghasilkan siri yang tidak lagi kelihatan seperti satu kursus. Inilah mod kegagalan yang cuba dihalang oleh kontrak, dan itulah sebabnya blok gaya dan skrip templat disalin, bukan diperihalkan.

Why fourteen screens, each doing one thingMengapa empat belas skrin, setiap satu satu tugas

The spine is not an arbitrary number. Segmenting material so that each screen carries a single job keeps the load imposed by presentation low enough that the learner's effort goes into building the schema you are teaching rather than into holding the page together.1 A screen that does three jobs is not richer; it is more expensive to read. The same logic governs the build: one session, one unit.

Tulang belakang ini bukan nombor sembarangan. Membahagikan bahan supaya setiap skrin membawa satu tugas sahaja mengekalkan beban yang dikenakan oleh persembahan cukup rendah sehingga usaha pelajar tertumpu kepada membina skema yang anda ajarkan dan bukan kepada menyatukan halaman itu.1 Skrin yang melakukan tiga tugas bukannya lebih kaya; ia lebih mahal untuk dibaca. Logik yang sama mengawal pembinaan: satu sesi, satu unit.

Core Concept · Konsep TerasKonsep Teras

The artefact is copied; the contract is obeyed

Artifak disalin; kontrak dipatuhi

DefinitionTakrifan

The golden template is one finished unit whose styling and behaviour every sibling inherits byte for byte. The Structure Contract is a short description of that template — class names, screen order, function signatures — used when the file itself cannot be attached. The template is the artefact; the contract is the specification of the artefact. Drift happens when a session is given neither and asked to "keep the same style".

Templat emas ialah satu unit siap yang gaya dan tingkah lakunya diwarisi setiap adik-beradik bait demi bait. Kontrak Struktur ialah huraian ringkas templat itu — nama kelas, susunan skrin, tandatangan fungsi — digunakan apabila fail itu sendiri tidak boleh dilampirkan. Templat ialah artifak; kontrak ialah spesifikasi artifak itu. Penyimpangan berlaku apabila sesi tidak diberi kedua-duanya dan disuruh "kekalkan gaya yang sama".

❌ “Keep the same styling”

❌ “Kekalkan gaya yang sama”

An instruction to preserve an impression. The model rewrites the CSS in its own words, arrives at something that looks close, and the next unit rewrites that. Three generations later the family resemblance is gone.

Arahan untuk mengekalkan tanggapan. Model menulis semula CSS dengan perkataannya sendiri, menghasilkan sesuatu yang kelihatan hampir sama, dan unit berikutnya menulis semula yang itu pula. Tiga generasi kemudian, persamaan keluarga itu hilang.

✅ “Copy these two blocks verbatim”

✅ “Salin dua blok ini bait demi bait”

An instruction about bytes, which is checkable. Combined with a whitelist of the five things that may change, it makes every sibling a true copy — and makes any deviation a diff you can see rather than a feeling you cannot.

Arahan tentang bait, yang boleh disemak. Digabungkan dengan senarai putih lima perkara yang boleh berubah, ia menjadikan setiap adik-beradik salinan sebenar — dan menjadikan sebarang penyelewengan sebagai perbezaan yang boleh dilihat, bukan perasaan yang tidak dapat dipastikan.

Whitelist, not blacklistSenarai putih, bukan senarai hitam

Listing what may change is stronger than listing what may not, because the whitelist is finite and the blacklist never is. Five items — title, accent rule, brand text, teasers, content — and everything absent from that list is invariant by default.

Menyenaraikan apa yang boleh berubah lebih kukuh daripada menyenaraikan apa yang tidak boleh, kerana senarai putih itu terhingga manakala senarai hitam tidak pernah terhingga. Lima item — tajuk, peraturan aksen, teks jenama, penggoda, kandungan — dan segala yang tiada dalam senarai itu adalah tak berubah secara lalai.

Diagram · RajahRajah

Five things change. Everything else is frozen.

Lima perkara berubah. Selebihnya beku.

Every sibling unit is the same file with five substitutions. If a diff between two units shows anything outside the amber column, something drifted.

Setiap unit adik-beradik ialah fail yang sama dengan lima penggantian. Jika perbezaan antara dua unit menunjukkan apa-apa di luar lajur ambar, sesuatu telah terpesong.

The asymmetry is deliberate: six frozen categories described loosely, five changes described exactly. Precision belongs where the decisions are.Ketidaksimetrian ini disengajakan: enam kategori beku diperihalkan secara longgar, lima perubahan diperihalkan dengan tepat. Ketepatan diperlukan di tempat keputusan dibuat.
Example with Variations · Contoh & VariasiContoh & Variasi

One loop, run eleven times

Satu gelung, dijalankan sebelas kali

Base caseKes asas

Open a fresh session. Paste P0, the spec card, the contract, the ledger so far, and one brief — roughly three pages. Attach the template. Ask for unit n. Receive one file plus a seven-line ledger entry. Save both, close the session. The loop takes about forty-five minutes per unit, most of it your reading rather than the model's writing, and it does not get slower at unit 11 than it was at unit 2 — which is the entire point.

Buka sesi baharu. Tampal P0, kad spesifikasi, kontrak, lejar setakat ini, dan satu ringkasan — kira-kira tiga halaman. Lampirkan templat. Minta unit n. Terima satu fail beserta catatan lejar tujuh baris. Simpan kedua-duanya, tutup sesi. Gelung ini mengambil kira-kira empat puluh lima minit setiap unit, kebanyakannya bacaan anda dan bukan penulisan model, dan ia tidak menjadi lebih perlahan pada unit 11 berbanding unit 2 — itulah keseluruhan intinya.

Variation A — the model truncates halfway through screen 9. Variasi A — model terpotong di pertengahan skrin 9. Switch to the split-output protocol: three chunks with explicit end markers, and reply only with the word CONTINUE between them. Anything else you type between chunks becomes new context and invites drift. Tukar kepada protokol pecahan keluaran: tiga bahagian dengan penanda akhir yang jelas, dan balas hanya dengan perkataan CONTINUE antaranya. Apa sahaja lain yang anda taip antara bahagian menjadi konteks baharu dan mengundang penyimpangan.
Variation B — you cannot attach the template. Variasi B — anda tidak boleh melampirkan templat. Paste CONTRACT.md instead and accept that minor CSS drift becomes possible. Compensate by auditing every second unit rather than every third. The contract is a fallback, not an equal substitute. Tampal CONTRACT.md sebagai ganti dan terima bahawa sedikit penyimpangan CSS menjadi mungkin. Imbangi dengan mengaudit setiap unit kedua dan bukan setiap unit ketiga. Kontrak ialah sandaran, bukan pengganti setara.
Variation C — the brief demands more than the hours allow. Variasi C — ringkasan menuntut lebih daripada jam yang dibenarkan. P5 instructs the model to trim and say so in a closing comment. Read those comments: three of them in a row means your unit map, not your material, is what needs fixing. P5 mengarahkan model memangkas dan menyatakannya dalam komen penutup. Baca komen itu: tiga kali berturut-turut bermakna peta unit anda, bukan bahan anda, yang perlu dibetulkan.

TASK: Build Unit 1 as a single self-contained HTML file. This becomes the
GOLDEN TEMPLATE for all units, so its CSS and JS must be generic enough to be
copied verbatim into every later unit.

TECHNICAL
- One file. Inline <style> and <script>. No external dependencies, no build
  step, no CDN, no frameworks. Must work when opened from a local disk.
- No localStorage or sessionStorage.
- Responsive to 380px. Print stylesheet that reveals all screens.
- WCAG AA contrast everywhere. Keyboard navigation. Semantic headings.

DUAL VIEW ENGINE
- Screen mode: one section visible at a time; arrow keys and Prev/Next; a
  progress bar and an "n / total" counter in a fixed top bar.
- Guide mode: toggled by a button and the G key; all screens shown as one
  scrollable document. This is what makes it a SIM, not lecture support.

LANGUAGE ENGINE (omit if monolingual)
- Every translatable node exists twice: <span lang="[L1]"> and <span lang="[L2]">.
- CSS alone hides the inactive twin. A toggle in the top bar calls setLang().

DESIGN TOKENS (:root custom properties)
ink, ink-soft, muted, bg, panel, line, accent, accent-2, accent-soft, amber
trio, green trio, red trio, radius, shadow, maxw, font, mono.
Per-unit theming happens ONLY through:

COMPONENT LIBRARY (define all in CSS even if unit 1 uses only some)

THE 14-SCREEN SPINE (this order, every unit, no exceptions)
 1 Cover . 2 Outcomes (3, with CLO chips + an assessment-alignment block)
 3 Hook (real story + factual anchor) . 4 Core concept (definition + contrast)
 5 Diagram (hand-authored inline SVG + caption) . 6 Example + 3 variations
 7 Practice Lab . 8 Applications (4 cards + local-lens block)
 9 Framework (named checklist) . 10 Self-check (3 questions)
11 Tools (4 cards, real links) . 12 Resources (2 columns, every item linked,
   plus a "branch into" row of pills) . 13 Learning network (back / forward
   columns, 3 external node actions, own-case assessment tie-in)
14 Closing (summary, memorable line, before-next-unit list, teaser)

SCREEN 7 - THE PRACTICE LAB
- A head with title, subtitle and a Copy button calling copyPrompt(this).
- A <pre><code> holding a copy-paste-ready prompt or protocol built around the
  LEARNER'S OWN case. Blanks wrapped in <span class="ph">[LIKE THIS]</span>,
  structural keywords in <span class="kw">.
- Two columns: left = line-by-line explanation of WHY each line is there;
  right = "Expected output (illustrative)" showing the SHAPE of a good answer
  without giving THE answer.
- A closing note reminding the learner the case and judgement are their own.

SVG RULES
Author it by hand. No placeholder rectangles labelled "diagram here". Every
label dark on light OR white on dark; never light grey on light. Visible stroke
on every shape. Under ~40 elements so it stays legible on a phone.

SELF-CHECK BEHAVIOUR
Each question carries data-answer="0..3". pick(el,i) marks correct/incorrect,
disables the group and reveals the matching feedback. Feedback for WRONG
answers must teach the distinction, not just say "incorrect".

CONTENT
Use the Unit 1 brief. Write in the register of the audience in SPEC - plain,
concrete, second-person, example-led. No filler, no marketing tone. If
bilingual, both languages carry the same substance; the second is a real
translation, not a stub.

Output the complete HTML file only.

📐 P4 — The Structure Contract
Run in the SAME session as P3, while the template is still in view.
TASK: Extract a STRUCTURE CONTRACT from the file you just produced.

The contract is what I will paste into future sessions INSTEAD of the whole
template. It must be under 70 lines, and must let a DIFFERENT model, with no
sight of the template file, produce a class-identical sibling unit.

Include:
1. The exact head requirements and the body attribute pattern.
2. The full list of CSS class names, grouped by component, each with a one-line
   note on its purpose. Names only - not the rules.
3. The 14 screen sections in order, with the required components inside each.
4. The exact JS function signatures that must exist, and what each does in one
   line: setLang(l), toggleMode(), go(n), show(i), pick(el,i), copyPrompt(btn).
5. The five things that change between units, and NOTHING else:
   title . the data-accent value and its one CSS rule . the "Unit n / N" brand
   text . the cover teasers and next-unit pointer . all screen content.
6. The invariants: the whole style block and the whole script block are copied
   VERBATIM, byte for byte.

Output the contract only, as markdown.

Why it is shaped this wayMengapa ia berbentuk begini

  • Test"A different model, with no sight of the file" is the acceptance test. If the contract only works for the model that wrote it, it is a memory aid, not a specification."Model berbeza, tanpa melihat fail" ialah ujian penerimaan. Jika kontrak hanya berfungsi untuk model yang menulisnya, ia alat bantu ingatan, bukan spesifikasi.
  • NamesClass names without rules is the whole compression trick: the names are what must match; the rules get copied.Nama kelas tanpa peraturan ialah keseluruhan helah pemampatan: nama itulah yang mesti sepadan; peraturan disalin.
  • 70The line cap is deliberate. A contract long enough to be inconvenient is a contract that stops getting pasted.Had baris itu disengajakan. Kontrak yang cukup panjang untuk menyusahkan ialah kontrak yang berhenti ditampal.

3. Top-bar brand text -> "Unit [n] / [N]"

WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE

CONTINUITY - use the LEDGER

- Screen 13 "feeds forward to" must name specific later units from the map.
- Do NOT reuse any hook story, primary case, tool or source already listed in
  the ledger. Check the ledger before choosing.
- Screen 9's framework must be new, and should visibly build on an earlier
  unit's framework.
- Screen 14's teaser must match the next unit's actual title.

QUALITY BARS
- The Practice Lab must exercise THIS unit's specific method, and be genuinely
  runnable today - not a generic "analyse my case" prompt.
- The SVG must be authored for this unit's concept. No reuse of unit 1's shape.
- All 4 tool links and all 4 sources real and current. Mark [VERIFY] if unsure.
- If bilingual, both languages carry equal substance.
- Reading + activity load must fit this unit's independent-learning hours. If
  the brief demands more than fits, trim and note it in a closing comment.

OUTPUT
The complete HTML file only. Then, AFTER the file, output the ledger entry in
exactly this form and nothing more:

accent: [three hex values]
hook: [one line - the story used]
framework: [the named framework introduced]
sources: [4 short citation keys]
tools: [4 tool names]
links-back: [units] | links-fwd: [units]

  • Src"Byte for byte" is not pedantry. Told merely to "keep the styling", models paraphrase CSS — and units slowly stop matching."Bait demi bait" bukan cerewet. Jika hanya disuruh "kekalkan gaya", model memparafrasa CSS — dan unit perlahan-lahan tidak lagi sepadan.
  • ChgAn explicit whitelist of five changes beats any list of prohibitions. Anything not on it is invariant by default.Senarai putih lima perubahan yang eksplisit mengatasi mana-mana senarai larangan. Apa sahaja yang tiada padanya adalah tak berubah secara lalai.
  • ContThis block is why the ledger exists. It is the only thing giving unit 9 accurate knowledge of unit 3 — for roughly 1% of the tokens.Blok inilah sebab lejar wujud. Ia satu-satunya perkara yang memberi unit 9 pengetahuan tepat tentang unit 3 — dengan kira-kira 1% token.
  • LoadTying content volume to the approved learning hours keeps the material honest against the workload the curriculum promised.Mengikat jumlah kandungan kepada jam pembelajaran yang diluluskan memastikan bahan jujur terhadap beban kerja yang dijanjikan kurikulum.
  • OutRequesting the ledger entry after the file means the model writes it having just built the unit — so it is accurate rather than predicted.Meminta catatan lejar selepas fail bermakna model menulisnya sebaik selesai membina unit — jadi ia tepat, bukan ramalan.

Betulkan templat, jalankan semula gelung, dan pembetulan itu merebak — kerana tiada apa di hilir yang menyimpang.
Local lens · MalaysiaLensa tempatan · Malaysia

For bilingual delivery the contract earns its keep twice over. The language-twin pattern — every visible string existing once per language, with CSS alone choosing which is shown — is easy to state in sixty lines and almost impossible to maintain by convention. Put it in the contract and every unit inherits it; leave it to good intentions and unit 8 will quietly ship a Malay block that is a stub of its English twin. Audit for that specifically: a second-language block much shorter than the first is not a translation.

Bagi penyampaian dwibahasa, kontrak ini berbaloi dua kali ganda. Corak kembar bahasa — setiap rentetan yang kelihatan wujud sekali bagi setiap bahasa, dengan CSS sahaja memilih yang mana ditunjukkan — mudah dinyatakan dalam enam puluh baris dan hampir mustahil dikekalkan melalui kebiasaan semata-mata. Letakkannya dalam kontrak dan setiap unit mewarisinya; serahkan kepada niat baik dan unit 8 akan senyap-senyap menghantar blok Melayu yang hanya tempelan kepada kembar Inggerisnya. Auditlah perkara itu secara khusus: blok bahasa kedua yang jauh lebih pendek daripada yang pertama bukanlah terjemahan.

Practical Framework · Kerangka AmaliKerangka Amali

The loop, as a checklist you can run from memory

Gelung itu, sebagai senarai semak yang boleh anda jalankan dari ingatan

Five steps per unit. Once these are habit, a unit takes about forty-five minutes and the eleventh is no harder than the second.

Lima langkah setiap unit. Setelah ini menjadi tabiat, satu unit mengambil kira-kira empat puluh lima minit dan yang kesebelas tidak lebih sukar daripada yang kedua.

1
Open fresh. A new session, every time. Paste P0, spec, contract, ledger, and only this unit's brief. Attach the template. Buka baharu. Sesi baharu, setiap kali. Tampal P0, spesifikasi, kontrak, lejar, dan hanya ringkasan unit ini. Lampirkan templat.
2
Ask for one unit. Never two. If the output truncates, switch to three chunks and reply only CONTINUE. Minta satu unit. Jangan dua. Jika keluaran terpotong, tukar kepada tiga bahagian dan balas CONTINUE sahaja.
3
Open it and click everything. Both languages, every tab, every self-check option — including the wrong ones, whose feedback must teach rather than scold. Buka dan klik semuanya. Kedua-dua bahasa, setiap tab, setiap pilihan semakan kendiri — termasuk yang salah, yang maklum balasnya mesti mengajar dan bukan menegur.
4
Append the ledger entry. Seven lines, unedited, never rewritten. This is the only memory the next unit will have of this one. Tambah catatan lejar. Tujuh baris, tanpa suntingan, jangan ditulis semula. Inilah satu-satunya ingatan yang akan dimiliki unit seterusnya tentang unit ini.
5
Close the session. Not minimise — close. A session left open is a session you will be tempted to reuse. Tutup sesi itu. Bukan minimumkan — tutup. Sesi yang dibiarkan terbuka ialah sesi yang anda akan tergoda untuk guna semula.
Split-output markersPenanda pecahan keluaran

CHUNK 1 = doctype through the end of the style block · CHUNK 2 = body through the end of screen 7 · CHUNK 3 = screen 8 through the closing tag. Each ends with a comment marker you delete after stitching in a text editor.

CHUNK 1 = doctype hingga akhir blok gaya · CHUNK 2 = body hingga akhir skrin 7 · CHUNK 3 = skrin 8 hingga tag penutup. Setiap satu berakhir dengan penanda komen yang anda padam selepas mencantum dalam penyunting teks.

Formative Check · Semakan FormatifSemakan Formatif

Quick check — instant feedback

Semakan pantas — maklum balas segera

No grade, just for learning.Tiada markah — hanya untuk pembelajaran.

1. Why are P3 and P4 the one pair of prompts deliberately run in the same session?1. Mengapa P3 dan P4 ialah satu-satunya pasangan gesaan yang sengaja dijalankan dalam sesi yang sama?
✓ Exactly. P4 is the one task where the artefact you are describing is already in view, so reusing the session is correct rather than risky. Every other prompt starts fresh.✓ Tepat. P4 ialah satu-satunya tugas yang artifaknya sudah berada dalam pandangan, jadi menggunakan semula sesi itu adalah betul dan bukan berisiko. Setiap gesaan lain bermula baharu.
It is not about convenience or dependency order. P4 asks the model to describe a file it just wrote — the only moment in the whole chain when the thing being described is already in context.Ia bukan tentang kemudahan atau susunan kebergantungan. P4 meminta model memerihalkan fail yang baru ditulisnya — satu-satunya saat dalam keseluruhan rantaian apabila perkara yang diperihalkan sudah berada dalam konteks.
2. You diff unit 5 against the template and find the CSS reformatted with different indentation but identical rules. Is that acceptable?2. Anda membandingkan unit 5 dengan templat dan mendapati CSS diformat semula dengan inden berbeza tetapi peraturannya sama. Adakah itu boleh diterima?
✓ Right. This unit renders fine, but the model regenerated the block instead of copying it — so the next unit will regenerate from this version. That is exactly how drift starts, one harmless-looking generation at a time.✓ Betul. Unit ini dipaparkan dengan baik, tetapi model menjana semula blok itu dan bukan menyalinnya — jadi unit seterusnya akan menjana semula daripada versi ini. Begitulah tepatnya penyimpangan bermula, satu penjanaan yang kelihatan tidak berbahaya pada satu masa.
Look past the rendering. Identical output today, but the model clearly regenerated rather than copied — and the next unit inherits that regenerated version, not the original. Three units later the rules themselves will have moved.Lihat melangkaui paparan. Keluaran sama hari ini, tetapi model jelas menjana semula dan bukan menyalin — dan unit seterusnya mewarisi versi yang dijana semula itu, bukan yang asal. Tiga unit kemudian, peraturannya sendiri akan berubah.
3. Between two chunks of a split output, what should you type?3. Antara dua bahagian keluaran yang dipecahkan, apa yang patut anda taip?
✓ Yes. Anything you add between chunks becomes new context competing with the instructions already given — the most common way a carefully set-up session drifts at the halfway mark.✓ Ya. Apa sahaja yang anda tambah antara bahagian menjadi konteks baharu yang bersaing dengan arahan yang telah diberikan — cara paling lazim sesi yang disediakan dengan teliti terpesong di pertengahan jalan.
Adding reminders feels helpful but is not. Every extra token between chunks competes with the instructions already in context; the protocol works precisely because it adds nothing.Menambah peringatan terasa membantu tetapi sebenarnya tidak. Setiap token tambahan antara bahagian bersaing dengan arahan yang sudah ada dalam konteks; protokol ini berkesan justeru kerana ia tidak menambah apa-apa.

Wrap-up · RumusanRumusan

Unit 3 in one breath

Unit 3 dalam satu nafas

Design one unit properly and every later unit is a copy with five substitutions. The template is copied byte for byte; the contract describes it in under seventy lines for when the file cannot be attached; and the whitelist names the five things that may change so that everything else is frozen by default. Drift is not a single bad output — it is a series of locally reasonable rewrites, which is why the instruction has to be about bytes rather than about style.

Reka bentuk satu unit dengan betul dan setiap unit kemudian ialah salinan dengan lima penggantian. Templat disalin bait demi bait; kontrak memerihalkannya dalam kurang tujuh puluh baris untuk keadaan fail tidak boleh dilampirkan; dan senarai putih menamakan lima perkara yang boleh berubah supaya selebihnya beku secara lalai. Penyimpangan bukan satu keluaran buruk — ia siri penulisan semula yang munasabah secara setempat, dan itulah sebabnya arahan mesti tentang bait dan bukan tentang gaya.
