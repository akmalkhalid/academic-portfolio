The Hook · Why It MattersPencetus · Mengapa Ia Penting

“Generate my 12-week course.” The first three weeks were good.

“Jana kursus 12 minggu saya.” Tiga minggu pertama memang bagus.

It is the most natural request in the world, and it fails in the most predictable way. Week 1 arrives rich and well-structured. Week 4 has quietly lost two of the required sections. Week 7 repeats Week 2's case study. By Week 9 the colour scheme has drifted, the outcome verbs have slid back to “understand”, and the model is confidently citing a paper that does not exist. Nothing announced the failure — the output kept looking fluent the whole way down.

Ia permintaan paling semula jadi di dunia, dan ia gagal dengan cara yang paling boleh dijangka. Minggu 1 tiba dengan kaya dan tersusun. Minggu 4 diam-diam kehilangan dua bahagian yang diwajibkan. Minggu 7 mengulang kajian kes Minggu 2. Menjelang Minggu 9 skema warna sudah terpesong, kata kerja hasil pembelajaran kembali kepada “memahami”, dan model dengan yakin memetik kertas kerja yang tidak wujud. Tiada apa mengumumkan kegagalan itu — hasilnya kekal kelihatan lancar sepanjang jalan.

The arithmetic behind the failureAritmetik di sebalik kegagalan

A single weekly deck in the reference course is roughly 800 lines of HTML. Twelve of them is around 10,000 lines — far beyond what any model will emit in one response, and far beyond the point where recall stays reliable. Research on long-context behaviour finds that models retrieve information most accurately when it sits at the very beginning or very end of the context, and measurably worse when it sits in the middle.1 Your Week 3 design rules are exactly that middle.

Satu deck mingguan dalam kursus rujukan ialah kira-kira 800 baris HTML. Dua belas daripadanya menjadi sekitar 10,000 baris — jauh melebihi apa yang mana-mana model akan keluarkan dalam satu respons, dan jauh melepasi titik di mana ingatannya kekal boleh dipercayai. Kajian tentang konteks panjang mendapati model mendapatkan semula maklumat paling tepat apabila ia berada di awal atau di hujung konteks, dan lebih lemah apabila ia berada di tengah.1 Peraturan reka bentuk Minggu 3 anda berada tepat di tengah itu.

Core Concept · Konsep TerasKonsep Teras

Separate the writer from the memory

Pisahkan penulis daripada ingatan

DefinitionTakrifan

A Self-Instructional Material (SIM) is learning material that can teach without a teacher present: it states its own outcomes, motivates, explains, demonstrates, gives the learner something to do, checks understanding with immediate feedback, and points forward. A prompt chain is the method used here to produce one: the AI supplies the writing, while you supply the project's memory in four small files it re-reads each session.

Sebuah Bahan Pembelajaran Kendiri (SIM) ialah bahan pembelajaran yang boleh mengajar tanpa kehadiran pengajar: ia menyatakan hasil pembelajarannya sendiri, memotivasi, menerangkan, menunjuk cara, memberi pelajar sesuatu untuk dilakukan, menyemak kefahaman dengan maklum balas segera, dan menunjuk ke hadapan. Rantaian gesaan ialah kaedah yang digunakan di sini untuk menghasilkannya: AI membekalkan penulisan, manakala anda membekalkan ingatan projek dalam empat fail kecil yang dibacanya semula setiap sesi.

❌ The monolithic prompt

❌ Gesaan monolitik

One giant instruction, one long conversation, twelve outputs. Memory lives in the chat history — which is exactly the thing that degrades. Nothing is auditable, nothing is reusable, and a single bad turn poisons everything after it.

Satu arahan gergasi, satu perbualan panjang, dua belas hasil. Ingatan berada dalam sejarah sembang — iaitu perkara yang tepat merosot. Tiada apa boleh diaudit, tiada apa boleh diguna semula, dan satu giliran buruk meracuni semua yang selepasnya.

✅ The prompt chain

✅ Rantaian gesaan

Ten small prompts, a fresh session per unit, and four files you own. Memory lives on your disk, in text you can read and correct. Any model can pick up the work; a bad output is thrown away, not inherited.

Sepuluh gesaan kecil, satu sesi baharu bagi setiap unit, dan empat fail milik anda. Ingatan berada pada cakera anda, dalam teks yang boleh anda baca dan betulkan. Mana-mana model boleh menyambung kerja itu; hasil yang buruk dibuang, bukan diwarisi.

The compression rulePeraturan pemampatan

Never paste an artefact when a description of the artefact will do. The 800-line template becomes a 60-line contract. Eleven finished units become a 70-line ledger. Three curriculum documents become a one-page spec. Roughly 97% fewer tokens, with no loss of the information that governs the next unit.

Jangan sekali-kali menampal artifak apabila huraian artifak itu memadai. Templat 800 baris menjadi kontrak 60 baris. Sebelas unit siap menjadi lejar 70 baris. Tiga dokumen kurikulum menjadi spesifikasi satu halaman. Kira-kira 97% lebih sedikit token, tanpa kehilangan maklumat yang mengawal unit berikutnya.

One method, four very different courses

Satu kaedah, empat kursus yang sangat berbeza

Base case · the reference implementationKes asas · pelaksanaan rujukan

A 2-credit general-education course, 12 weeks, 80 notional hours, bilingual, taught fully online. Two course outcomes — one cognitive at analysis level, one affective at organisation level. Assessment is a reflective report, a portfolio and two presentations; there is no examination. Learners come from every faculty and cannot be assumed to code. The spine that emerged: 14 screens per week, an interactive prompt lab in the middle, and one real case chosen by each learner in Week 1 and carried to Week 12.

Kursus pendidikan umum 2 kredit, 12 minggu, 80 jam nosional, dwibahasa, diajar sepenuhnya dalam talian. Dua hasil pembelajaran kursus — satu kognitif pada aras analisis, satu afektif pada aras organisasi. Pentaksiran ialah laporan refleksi, portfolio dan dua pembentangan; tiada peperiksaan. Pelajar datang dari semua fakulti dan tidak boleh diandaikan tahu mengatur cara. Tulang belakang yang terhasil: 14 skrin seminggu, satu makmal gesaan interaktif di tengah, dan satu kes sebenar yang dipilih setiap pelajar pada Minggu 1 dan dibawa hingga Minggu 12.

Variation A — a 6-week laboratory course. Variasi A — kursus makmal 6 minggu. The spine shortens to 10 screens and the Prompt Lab becomes a Bench Protocol: a printable step-list with safety notes, a predicted-result box, and a space for the learner's actual result. Everything else holds. The lesson: the component that changes is the practice slide, not the structure around it. Tulang belakang dipendekkan kepada 10 skrin dan Makmal Gesaan menjadi Protokol Bangku: senarai langkah boleh cetak dengan nota keselamatan, kotak keputusan dijangka, dan ruang untuk keputusan sebenar pelajar. Selebihnya kekal. Pengajarannya: komponen yang berubah ialah slaid amali, bukan struktur di sekelilingnya.
Variation B — a postgraduate seminar, monolingual, 14 weeks. Variasi B — seminar pascasiswazah, ekabahasa, 14 minggu. Drop the language twins from the contract and the file halves in size. Raise the outcome verbs to evaluation and creation level, and replace the three-item formative quiz with a position statement the learner drafts and then argues against. The lesson: taxonomy level drives the self-check format2 — multiple choice cannot assess synthesis. Buang kembar bahasa daripada kontrak dan saiz fail berkurang separuh. Tingkatkan kata kerja hasil kepada aras penilaian dan penciptaan, dan gantikan kuiz formatif tiga item dengan pernyataan pendirian yang dirangka pelajar kemudian dihujah balas sendiri. Pengajarannya: aras taksonomi menentukan format semakan kendiri2 — aneka pilihan tidak boleh mentaksir sintesis.
Variation C — professional CPD with no accreditation paperwork. Variasi C — CPD profesional tanpa dokumen akreditasi. There is no SLT sheet and no Table 4, so P1 has nothing to compress. Write the Spec Card by hand instead — it is only a page — and derive outcomes from the competency framework the profession already publishes. The lesson: the spec card is mandatory; the documents that feed it are not. Tiada helaian SLT dan tiada Jadual 4, jadi P1 tiada apa untuk dimampatkan. Tulis Kad Spesifikasi secara manual sebaliknya — ia hanya satu halaman — dan terbitkan hasil pembelajaran daripada rangka kerja kompetensi yang telah diterbitkan profesion itu. Pengajarannya: kad spesifikasi adalah wajib; dokumen yang menyuapnya tidak.

🧭 P0 — The Role Card
Paste at the top of EVERY session. This is the constitution.
You are an instructional designer and front-end developer producing
SELF-INSTRUCTIONAL MATERIALS (SIMs) for a university course. You write both
the pedagogy and the code. Do not ask permission to begin; produce the artefact.

PEDAGOGICAL NON-NEGOTIABLES

1. OUTCOME-BASED EDUCATION (constructive alignment)
   - Each unit states 3 outcomes: [observable verb at the mapped taxonomy
     level] + [object] + [context].
   - Each outcome shows a chip mapping it to a Course Learning Outcome and
     its code (e.g. "CLO1 . C4").
   - Each outcome must be traceable to an activity IN the unit AND to an
     assessment. No activity and no assessment means delete the outcome.
   - Banned verbs: understand, know, be aware of, appreciate, learn about.

2. CONNECTIVISM - instantiated, not merely mentioned. Every unit contains:
   - BACKWARD links: named prior units this one depends on.
   - FORWARD links: named later units this one feeds.
   - EXTERNAL NODES: 3+ concrete acts of connecting beyond the course.
   - LIVE SOURCES: current, dated, linked (DOI / arXiv / official URL).
   - DIVERGENCE: one genuinely open dispute, strongest case on each side.
   - PERSONAL NODE: the learner's own case is the connection point.

3. SELF-INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGN (no teacher present). Each unit provides:
   advance organiser -> outcomes -> hook -> concept + definition -> visual
   model -> worked example with variations -> guided activity with an
   illustrative expected output -> application -> self-check with immediate
   feedback on BOTH right and wrong answers -> summary -> forward pointer.

4. OPEN-ENDED, OWN-CASE ASSESSMENT
   Every activity and assessment requires the learner's OWN chosen real case.
   Never a fixed toy answer. State that no two learners should submit the
   same case. Formative MCQs are the one exception.

5. ACCURACY
   Every claim, statistic, case, law and citation must be real. If unsure a
   source exists, write [VERIFY] beside it. Never invent a DOI or an author.

OUTPUT DISCIPLINE
- Output the artefact only. No preamble, no summary of what you did.
- When asked for HTML, output ONE complete file, <!DOCTYPE html> to </html>.
- Never abbreviate with "... rest unchanged" or "[same as before]".
- On conflicting instructions, follow the STRUCTURE CONTRACT and flag the
  conflict in one HTML comment at the end of the file.

Working language for learner-facing text: [LANGUAGE(S)].

Acknowledge with the single word READY and wait.

Local lens · MalaysiaLensa tempatan · Malaysia

Practical Framework · Kerangka AmaliKerangka Amali

The Six-Rule Context Protocol

Protokol Konteks Enam Peraturan

Follow these six and the chain holds together on any model, including small local ones. Break rule 1 or rule 4 and it will not, no matter how large the context window.

Ikut keenam-enam ini dan rantaian ini akan bertahan pada mana-mana model, termasuk model tempatan yang kecil. Langgar peraturan 1 atau 4 dan ia tidak akan bertahan, betapa besar pun tetingkap konteksnya.

1
One session per artefact. One chat produces one deliverable, then you close it. Never build unit 4 in the session that built unit 3. Satu sesi bagi satu artifak. Satu sembang menghasilkan satu penyampaian, kemudian anda tutup. Jangan bina unit 4 dalam sesi yang membina unit 3.
2
The four files are the only memory. If a fact matters to a later unit and is not in SPEC, CONTRACT or LEDGER, it does not exist. Empat fail itu sahaja ingatannya. Jika sesuatu fakta penting untuk unit kemudian dan tiada dalam SPEC, CONTRACT atau LEDGER, ia tidak wujud.
3
Attach the template; never paste it. Use project files or a repo. Where attachment is impossible, paste the contract instead and expect minor drift. Lampirkan templat; jangan tampal. Gunakan fail projek atau repositori. Jika lampiran mustahil, tampal kontrak sebaliknya dan jangkakan sedikit penyimpangan.
4
Never ask for more than one unit. The output ceiling is about one unit per turn. Asking for two guarantees the second is truncated or shallow. Jangan minta lebih daripada satu unit. Had keluaran ialah kira-kira satu unit setiap giliran. Meminta dua menjamin yang kedua terpotong atau cetek.
5
Split the output when it truncates. Three chunks with explicit end markers; reply only with the word CONTINUE between them; stitch in an editor. Pecahkan keluaran apabila terpotong. Tiga bahagian dengan penanda akhir yang jelas; balas hanya dengan perkataan CONTINUE antaranya; cantumkan dalam penyunting.
6

Wrap-up · RumusanRumusan

Unit 1 in one breath

Unit 1 dalam satu nafas

A multi-unit course is too large for any single AI session, and it fails quietly rather than loudly: the output stays fluent while the structure, the sources and the outcome verbs drift. The fix is not a better prompt but a smaller job — compress the curriculum into four short files, build one unit per fresh session from about three pages of context, and let a seven-line ledger carry the continuity between them.

Kursus berbilang unit terlalu besar untuk mana-mana sesi AI tunggal, dan ia gagal secara senyap dan bukan lantang: hasilnya kekal lancar sementara struktur, sumber dan kata kerja hasil pembelajaran terpesong. Penyelesaiannya bukan gesaan yang lebih baik tetapi tugas yang lebih kecil — mampatkan kurikulum menjadi empat fail pendek, bina satu unit setiap sesi baharu daripada kira-kira tiga halaman konteks, dan biarkan lejar tujuh baris membawa kesinambungan antaranya.
