How your work is recorded

  1. Work through a session. Each thing you complete is recorded in an evidence ledger — press S at any time to see it.
  2. At the end, press Reveal my scorecard. Nothing is scored aloud before that.
  3. Use Copy my report code or Download report file to hand it back. That is the only way anything leaves the page.

Carrying Session A forward

Keep your Session A code. On slide 2 of Session B there is a box to paste it into, and both sessions then appear in one view with your self-rating compared against what you built. Entirely optional — Session B works on its own.
