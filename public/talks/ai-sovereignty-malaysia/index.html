Hook
19 May 2025 · Kuala Lumpur

A national AI launch that lasted 24 hours

13 May

US Commerce: using Huawei Ascend chips anywhere in the world may violate US export controls.

19 May

Deputy Minister launches Malaysia's "Strategic AI Infrastructure" on Ascend + DeepSeek — "first of its kind outside China".

20 May

Statement retracted. MITI: "not involved… reaffirms its sovereign right". Huawei: no Ascend chips sold in Malaysia.

If a non-superpower cannot choose its own chips for a national project, who actually holds sovereignty over its AI? That is the question for the next 15 minutes.
