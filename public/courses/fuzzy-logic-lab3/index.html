(a) The three variables

Calcification

Small calcium deposits seen as bright white specks on a mammogram. Most are harmless; fine, clustered micro-calcifications need a closer look.

Input · 0–10 · Less / Several / More

Mass

A space-occupying lesion visible in two mammogram views. Radiologists judge its size, shape, margin and density.

Input · 0–30 · Small / Medium / Large

Impression

The radiologist's overall conclusion from all findings (reported in practice as a BI-RADS category).

Output · −20 to 20 · Benign / Suspicious / Malignant

tipDefine the three variables, then the three impression terms (benign = not cancerous, suspicious = possibly cancerous, malignant = cancerous). This is a teaching model, not a clinical tool.

When it goes wrong

  

    
Juzzy shape looks odd
Values out of order, or a triangle given 4 numbers. Re-type ascending.

    
μ = 0.35, not 0.333
Check "several" is exactly (2, 5, 8).

    
Output stuck at the centre
No rule fired. Rule terms must match MF names exactly.

    
NameError in Colab
Run the imports and class cell first, top to bottom.

    
COG off by 0.05
Grid sampling (500 points). Exact areas give the true value.

    
MoM vs MM mixed up
MoM = middle of the plateau. MM = its first point.
