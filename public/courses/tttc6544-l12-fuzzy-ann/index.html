  • A s10 — μ² = 0.5 → μ = 0.707 → level ≈ 55 + 0.707·30 ≈ 76%.μ² = 0.5 → μ = 0.707 → paras ≈ 55 + 0.707·30 ≈ 76%.
  • A s13 — (1) rain ≤ 10, level ≥ 90 fires Light∧Danger → High. (2) Product lowers firing strengths (0.45 and 0.13); at 45/70 the risk drops slightly, 56.4 → 55.9.(1) hujan ≤ 10, paras ≥ 90 menyalakan Ringan∧Bahaya → Tinggi. (2) Hasil darab merendahkan kekuatan nyalaan (0.45 dan 0.13); pada 45/70 risiko turun sedikit, 56.4 → 55.9.
  • A s24 — Widest on the slopes midway between feet and peak; zero at the peak and far outside the support.Paling lebar pada cerun antara kaki dan puncak; sifar di puncak dan jauh di luar sokongan.
  • A s26 — Raising U lifts yᵣ (cautious experts pull towards High) while yₗ stays at 50: report the interval, prepare early.Menaikkan U mengangkat yᵣ (pakar berhati-hati menarik ke arah Tinggi) manakala yₗ kekal 50: laporkan selang, bersedia awal.
  • B s6 — e.g. AND: w₁ = w₂ = 1, b = −1.5; OR: b = −0.5; NAND: w = −1, b = 1.5; XOR impossible with one line.cth. AND: w₁ = w₂ = 1, b = −1.5; OR: b = −0.5; NAND: w = −1, b = 1.5; XOR mustahil dengan satu garis.

  • B s23 — Recall ≥ 95% holds up to a threshold of 0.44, which costs 31 false alarms out of 160 dry days (more if you go lower).Dapatan semula ≥ 95% kekal sehingga ambang 0.44, dengan kos 31 amaran palsu daripada 160 hari kering (lebih banyak jika lebih rendah).

🛡️ Safe & ethical AI use for this modulePenggunaan AI yang selamat & beretika untuk modul ini

SAFE

  • S — Sensitive? Remove names, IC numbers, addresses, faces, car plates. Prefer synthetic data (all labs use it).Sensitif? Buang nama, nombor KP, alamat, wajah, nombor plat. Utamakan data sintetik (semua makmal menggunakannya).
  • A — Approved? Use institution-approved tools and accounts; check the tool's data-retention setting.Diluluskan? Guna alat dan akaun yang diluluskan institusi; semak tetapan penyimpanan data alat.
  • F — Fair? Look for who is missing from the data and who bears the errors.Adil? Cari siapa yang tiada dalam data dan siapa yang menanggung kesilapan.
  • E — Explainable & escalated? Verify AI output (acceptance tests in every L1 prompt); a human signs off.Boleh diterang & dirujuk? Sahkan output AI (ujian penerimaan dalam setiap prom T1); manusia memberi kelulusan.

Academic integrityIntegriti akademik

Declare AI assistance in studio deliverables (tool, purpose, what you changed). AI may explain, draft and build demos; you remain responsible for correctness, citations and judgement. Follow Malaysia's AIGE principles and PDPA when real data is involved.Isytiharkan bantuan AI dalam hasil studio (alat, tujuan, apa yang anda ubah). AI boleh menerangkan, mendraf dan membina demo; anda kekal bertanggungjawab terhadap ketepatan, petikan dan pertimbangan. Patuhi prinsip AIGE dan PDPA Malaysia apabila data sebenar terlibat.
